## 1. Commercial Positioning Strategy

Dutch SMEs do not buy abstract "cybersecurity" or fear-mongering warnings. They buy:
1. **Financial Risk Protection:** Stopping fraudulent bank transfer requests and fake invoices sent in their name.
2. **Regulatory & Reputation Peace of Mind:** Demonstrating compliant customer data handling (AVG/GDPR) without legal headaches.
3. **Local Revenue Growth:** Faster mobile pages, working contact buttons, and top search positioning in Arnhem over local competitors.
4. **Massive Cost Savings:** Comparing an ongoing managed service of **€49 – €149/month** against hiring a junior employee (**€2,200+/month**).
