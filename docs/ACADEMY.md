# ARGUS Academy Syllabus

## Module 1: The Deterministic Evidence Pipeline
Learn how ARGUS captures raw HTTP/DNS telemetry, hashes it into an immutable SHA-256 evidence block, and evaluates it through 11 pure deterministic functions to produce a zero-false-positive finding.

## Module 2: The PolicyEngine & Anti-SSRF Defenses
Interactive demonstration of how the ARGUS PolicyEngine intercepts and blocks target URLs that attempt to route into RFC1918 private space, loopbacks, or cloud metadata endpoints.

## Module 3: AI at the Edge
Understand how the LLM operates as an untrusted presentation layer constrained by the Policy Gate, forcing every generated claim to cite an authentic evidence ID.

## Module 4: Proof of Remediation
A hands-on lab on exporting, fixing, and retesting a target to generate a cryptographically signed Ed25519 proof of remediation.
